# 📱 SUPER EASY: Install StudyOS on Your Phone

## 🎯 The Simple Truth

**Your phone needs ZERO commands. Nothing. Zip. Zilch.**

You only run commands on your Windows laptop. Then you get a link. Open the link on your phone. Tap install. Done.

---

## 🖥️ STEP 1: Deploy from Your Laptop (Do this ONCE)

### On your Windows laptop:

1. Open Command Prompt in your StudyOS folder
2. Type this and press Enter:
   ```
   deploy-netlify.bat
   ```
3. A browser window opens → Login with Google (free)
4. Wait 30 seconds
5. You'll see a URL like: `https://studyos-abc123.netlify.app`
6. **COPY THIS URL** (you need it for your phone)

**That's all you do on the laptop!**

---

## 📱 STEP 2: Install on Your Phone (NO COMMANDS!)

### On your Android phone:

1. **Get the URL to your phone** (pick one):
   - Email it to yourself
   - Send it via WhatsApp
   - Text it to yourself
   - Write it down and type it

2. **Open Chrome browser** on your phone

3. **Paste/type the URL** and go to it

4. **Wait 3 seconds** → You'll see a banner that says "Install StudyOS"

5. **Tap "Install Now"**

6. **Confirm** when it asks

7. **DONE!** ✨ StudyOS icon appears on your home screen!

## 🆘 Help! The Install Button Doesn't Show Up

**Try this:**
1. Make sure you're using **Chrome** browser
2. Clear Chrome cache: Settings → Apps → Chrome → Storage → Clear cache
3. Reload the page
4. Wait 3-5 seconds
5. Look for the banner at the bottom

**Still not working?**
1. Tap the **⋮** menu (three dots, top right)
2. Look for **"Install app"** or **"Add to Home screen"**
3. Tap it
4. Confirm
