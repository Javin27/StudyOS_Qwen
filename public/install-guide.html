Install on Your Phone in 2 Minutes

📱 Phone Installation

1

Get the Link from Your Laptop

Ask someone to run deploy-netlify.bat on their computer and send you the URL.

Or if you have the project:
Run deploy-netlify.bat yourself and copy the URL it shows.
2

Open the Link on Your Phone

Send the URL to your phone (email, WhatsApp, or text it to yourself), then open it in Chrome browser.

⚠️ Important: Use Chrome browser! Safari or other browsers won't show the install button.
3

Tap "Install Now"

Wait 3 seconds. You'll see a purple banner at the bottom that says "Install StudyOS".

Tap the "Install Now" button.

4

Confirm Installation

A popup will ask you to confirm. Tap "Install".

✅ Done!

StudyOS is now on your home screen!

❓ Troubleshooting

?

Install Button Not Showing?

Make sure you're using Chrome browser. Then:

1. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right)

2. Look for "Install app" or "Add to Home screen"

3. Tap it and confirm

?

Still Not Working?

Clear Chrome cache:

Settings → Apps → Chrome → Storage → Clear cache

Then reload the page and wait 5 seconds.

💡 Pro Tip

Once installed, StudyOS works completely offline. No internet needed! Your data is saved on your phone.
